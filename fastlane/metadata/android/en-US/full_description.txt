Sniffy is a fully offline satirical party game.

After choosing the number of players, optional profile values generate comedy aliases and entirely fictional centimetre scores. All lines are shown together before sequential countdowns and a small shovel animation begin.

Names or aliases and scores can be saved locally and shown in weekly or monthly rankings.

The displayed values have no medical, pharmacological or dosage-related meaning. Sniffy does not calculate substance amounts, tolerance or consumption recommendations.

No advertising, tracking, network permission or cloud services.
